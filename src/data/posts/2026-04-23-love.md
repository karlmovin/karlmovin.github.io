Do you really have to know someone to love them? You can love your child without knowing them. You can love your dog without knowing them. And do you really know people you feel love for? Yes, you know people you've known for 10 years. And you might love them. You might love your parents or your siblings whom you've shared your early years and your almost entire life. But do you really need to know a person to love them?
